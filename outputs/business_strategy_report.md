# SmartCommerce Business Strategy Report

Products analyzed: 1,350 | Avg rating: 4.09 | Avg discount: 46.7% | Categories: 4

## Prioritized recommendations

1. **Pricing:** Discount depth is linked to lower ratings (corr -0.162); test discount levels by category before scaling.
2. **Promotions:** The 20-40% discount band drives the most reviews per product; anchor promotional campaigns around this range.
3. **Category focus:** Double down on Electronics, which leads customer engagement, and use OfficeProducts (highest rated) as the quality benchmark.
4. **Category risk:** Audit Home&Kitchen: lowest average rating; review top complaints and supplier quality.
5. **Customer experience:** 5.0% of products rate below 3.5. Prioritize fixes for high-review, low-rating items, since they shape the most buyer impressions.
